<b>FlowLink</b> lets you connect your Android phone and Windows PC over local Wi-Fi or Tailscale without using cloud servers.

<b>Features:</b>
• <b>Clipboard Sync:</b> Copy text or links on your phone and paste directly on your PC, and vice versa.
• <b>File Sharing:</b> Send photos, documents, and large files directly between devices over your local network.
• <b>PC Remote Control:</b> Lock your PC screen, put it to sleep, or shut it down from your phone.
• <b>Tailscale Support:</b> Connect even when you're away from home using your private Tailscale network.
• <b>Private & Open Source:</b> No ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

<b>Requirements:</b>
Requires FlowLink Desktop on your Windows 10 or 11 PC.
