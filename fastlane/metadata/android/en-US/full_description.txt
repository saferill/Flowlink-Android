<b>FlowLink</b> seamlessly connects your Android smartphone with your Windows PC into a single unified workspace. Enjoy real-time synchronization, instant file sharing, and remote control over your local network and Tailscale mesh VPN without reliance on third-party cloud servers.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• <b>Instant Clipboard Sync:</b> Copy text, links, or snippets on your Android phone and paste immediately on your Windows PC (and vice versa) with sub-second latency.
• <b>Blazing-Fast File Transfer:</b> Wire-speed peer-to-peer file transfer. Adaptive socket buffers send photos and documents in milliseconds, and large videos at maximum Wi-Fi bandwidth.
• <b>Remote Power Controls:</b> Lock your PC screen, close all running apps, restart, or safely shut down your Windows computer directly from your phone.
• <b>Tailscale Mesh Support:</b> Automatic discovery and continuous connection across different networks using secure WireGuard-based Tailscale private IPs.
• <b>100% Private & Open Source:</b> End-to-end encrypted local connections. No telemetry, no advertisements, and no third-party tracking.
• <b>Banking App Friendly:</b> Designed with strict privacy standards—no dangerous accessibility overlay permissions or intrusive SMS interception.

<b>Requirements:</b>
Requires the companion <b>FlowLink Desktop</b> app installed on your Windows 10/11 PC.
Source code & desktop client: https://github.com/saferill/Flowlink-Desktop
